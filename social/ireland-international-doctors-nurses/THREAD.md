## Post 1/8  [192 chars]  → image: per-person.png

Half of Ireland’s nurses and more than four in ten of its doctors trained abroad.

Without them, each nurse or midwife would serve 130 people instead of 63, and each doctor 433 instead of 245.

Alt: Dot charts titled If every clinician trained abroad left Ireland. One practising nurse or midwife for every 63 people with all staff, rising to one for every 130 with Irish-trained staff only (2025). One clinically active doctor for every 245 people, rising to one for every 433 with Irish-trained doctors only (2025). Each dot is one person. Based on the CSO population estimate for April 2025, NMBI State of the Register 2025 and the Medical Council Workforce Intelligence Report 2025.

## Post 2/8  [236 chars]  → image: education.png

Of 86,948 practising nurses and midwives in June 2025, 42,432 (49%) were educated outside the EU and 41,938 (48%) in Ireland.

Among those who work directly with patients, nurses educated outside the EU are the larger group: 50% to 47%.

Alt: Two donut charts titled Where Ireland’s nurses and midwives were educated, 2025. All practising registrants, 86,948: 48% educated in Ireland (41,938), 49% outside the EU (42,432), 3% elsewhere in the EU (2,578). Patient-facing registrants, 79,194: 47% Ireland (37,041), 50% outside the EU (39,760), 3% elsewhere in the EU (2,393). Source: NMBI State of the Register 2025, Table 2.

## Post 3/8  [227 chars]

India is the largest single source. More than one in five registered nurses and midwives is an Indian national: 20,419 in 2025.

In 2023–24, 3,717 new registrants trained in India, more than twice the 1,569 from Irish colleges.

## Post 5/8  [198 chars]

Hospitals run on two tracks.

68% of junior doctors on training schemes, the route to consultant posts, qualified in Ireland. 67% of junior doctors outside those schemes are international graduates.

## Post 6/8  [196 chars]  → image: permits.png

Recruitment is falling. Health and social work employment permits rose every year to 12,501 in 2024, then fell 36% to 7,948 in 2025.

New non-EU nurse registrants fell 38% in the year to May 2025.

Alt: Line chart titled Employment permits issued, 2021 to 2025, showing health and social work permits: 5,793 in 2021, 9,791 in 2022, 10,037 in 2023, 12,501 in 2024, then down 36% to 7,948 in 2025. All-sector totals for those years were 16,275, 39,955, 30,981, 39,390 and 31,044. Source: Department of Enterprise, Employment Permit Statistics, permits issued by economic sector.

With fewer clinicians arriving, keeping those already here matters more.
