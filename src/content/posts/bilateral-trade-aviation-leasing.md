Economic relations between Ireland and India have expanded rapidly, reaching an estimated €5.8 billion in total bilateral trade in goods and services in 2025.

## Aviation Financing & Aircraft Leasing

Dublin is the undisputed global capital of aircraft leasing, managing over 60% of the world's leased commercial aircraft fleet. With Indian carriers placing historic, multi-hundred aircraft orders to meet exploding domestic passenger demand, Dublin-based lessors (AerCap, SMBC Aviation Capital, Avolon) have become critical financing partners for Indian aviation.

## Pharma and Software Exchanges

Ireland's world-leading biopharmaceutical cluster exports advanced therapeutics, active ingredients, and medical devices to India, while Indian generic manufacturers and IT service titans maintain substantial European operational hubs in Dublin and Shannon.

With active Enterprise Ireland trade missions and growing university research partnerships, the bilateral economic corridor is poised for sustained double-digit growth.
