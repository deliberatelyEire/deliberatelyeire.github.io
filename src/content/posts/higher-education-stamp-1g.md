Over 9,000 Indian students currently pursue postgraduate taught (MSc/MA) and doctoral (PhD) degrees across Irish universities, including UCD, Trinity College Dublin, University of Galway, and UCC.

Ireland's attractive two-year post-study work visa (Third Level Graduate Scheme - Stamp 1G) has made Irish higher education an increasingly sought-after destination for top Indian talent in data analytics, computer science, biotechnology, and financial engineering.

This student cohort has created vibrant cultural bridges on Irish campuses and provides a crucial pipeline of high-skilled researchers driving Irish scientific output.
