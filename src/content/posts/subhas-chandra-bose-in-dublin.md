In February 1936, Subhas Chandra Bose spent ten days in Dublin on a personal fact-finding mission. He held private, unpublicized meetings with President of the Executive Council Éamon de Valera, prominent Fianna Fáil ministers, and republican leaders.

Bose studied the administrative mechanisms through which the Irish Free State had dismantled British imperial prerogatives (such as the Oath of Allegiance and the Governor-General's office), taking detailed notes that influenced his subsequent strategic thinking on sovereign statehood.
