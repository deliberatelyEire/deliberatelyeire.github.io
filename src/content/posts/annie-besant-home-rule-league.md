In 1916, as Ireland erupted in the Easter Rising, Annie Besant and Lokmanya Bal Gangadhar Tilak launched the All India Home Rule League. Besant, deeply versed in the political tactics of Irish leaders Isaac Butt and Charles Stewart Parnell, explicitly imported the organizational blueprint of Irish constitutional nationalism to India.

The Home Rule League mobilized hundreds of thousands of Indians through weekly pamphlets, public lectures, and local branch networks, forever shifting the Indian independence movement from elite drawing rooms into mass popular agitation.
